Seminario Teoria dei Numeri 2001-2002

     

(Venerdì, ore 16:30, Aula Seminari, Università di Roma “Tor Vergata”)




Speakers

  • December 7, 2001: Joshua Pelosi (Università di Roma “La Sapienza”): Un metodo per contare i punti di una curva iperellittica su un campo finito (secondo Kedlaya)
  • December 14, 2001: Rania Wazir (Brown University): Mordell-Weil Groups on Elliptic Fibrations
  • December 21, 2001: Evelina Viada-Aehle (ETH Zürich): Intersecando una curva e sottogruppi algebrici
  • March 8, 2002: Julio Fernandez (UPC Barcelona): A modular approach to quadratic Q-curves, realizing octahedral extensions of Q
  • March 15, 2002: René Schoof (Università di Roma “Tor Vergata”): Il gruppo di Arakelov-Picard
  • March 22, 2002: Riccardo Re (Università di Catania): Operatore di Cartier sulle curve
  • March 28, 2002: Marina Monsurrò (EPFL Lausanne): Razionalità dei gruppi aggiunti, R-equivalenza e invarianti coomologici
  • April 12, 2002: Francis Gardeyn (ETH Zürich): New criteria for Anderson uniformizability of t-motives
  • April 19, 2002: Bruno Chiarellotto (Università di Padova): Regolatori sintomici
  • May 3, 2002: Andrea Bandini (Università di Roma “Tor Vergata”): Teoria di Iwasawa per Zp-estensioni multiple.
  • May 10, 2002: Takakazu Satoh (Saitama University, Tokyo): An asymptotically fast norm computation algorithm for unramified extensions of local fields.
  • May 17, 2002: John McKay (Concordia University, Montreal): Generalizing the elliptic modular function   j and its properties.
  • May 24, 2002: Gerard van der Geer (Universiteit van Amsterdam): An invariant of varieties in positive characteristic.
  • May 31, 2002: Jean-François Mestre (Université de Paris VII): Application of the arithmetic-geometric mean and its generalizations to the computation of the number of points of curves of genus 1 and 2 over a finite field of characteristic 2
  • June 7, 2002: Kiran Kedlaya (University of California, Berkeley): Computing zeta functions with cohomology
